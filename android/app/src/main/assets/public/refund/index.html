01. Merchant of Record Statement (Dodo Payments)

All paid subscriptions on Kinich (https://kinich.app), operated by Kinich, are sold and processed by our authorized Merchant of Record, Dodo Payments ("Dodo"). When you purchase a subscription, your payment transaction is processed by Dodo Payments, which handles payment collection, applicable tax/GST/VAT compliance, invoicing, and refund disbursements back to your original payment method.

03. How to Cancel Your Subscription

What Happens After You Cancel
Cancellation stops all future automatic renewals immediately. You will continue to enjoy full access to your paid Spark or Fire plan features until the end of your current paid billing period, after which your account automatically transitions back to the Free Tier.

You may cancel your recurring subscription at any time without penalty using any of the following methods:

  • In-App Self-Service: Open Kinich → Settings → Plans & Pricing and click "Manage / Cancel Subscription".
  • Dodo Payments Customer Portal: Click the "Manage Subscription" link in any email receipt or invoice sent to you by Dodo Payments.
  • By Email: Email kinich.app@kinich.app from your registered account email address with the subject "Cancel Subscription".

04. Refund Policy & Eligibility Windows

We stand behind the quality of Kinich and offer transparent refund terms:

  • 7-Day First-Purchase Satisfaction Guarantee: If you are a first-time subscriber to Spark or Fire and are not satisfied for any reason, you may request a 100% full refund within 7 calendar days of your initial purchase — no questions asked (limited to one first-purchase refund per customer).
  • Unintended Renewal Refunds (48-Hour Window): If you forgot to cancel before a monthly or annual renewal date, you may request a full refund of the renewal charge within 48 hours of the renewal charge, provided your account has not consumed paid plan usage during the new billing cycle.
  • Billing Errors, Duplicate Charges & Extended Outages: We always provide a 100% full or pro-rata refund in the event of an accidental duplicate charge, a verifiable technical billing error, or a continuous service-wide outage of Kinich exceeding 72 consecutive hours.

05. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are not provided in the following circumstances:

  • Requests submitted after the 7-day window on an initial purchase or after the 48-hour window on a renewal (except for billing errors or extended outages);
  • Partial-month or unused-time prorated refunds when you cancel mid-cycle after the refund window has closed (instead, your access continues through the end of the billing period);
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for violations of our Acceptable Use Policy (/acceptable-use) or Terms of Service (/terms);
  • Repeat refund requests from a customer who has already received a first-purchase refund on a prior subscription.

06. How to Request a Refund & Processing Timeline

To request a refund, please email kinich.app@kinich.app (or submit a ticket on our Contact & Support page at /contact with the topic "Billing") and include: (1) the email address associated with your Kinich account, and (2) your Dodo Payments invoice or receipt number.

We review and respond to all refund requests within 2 business days. Once approved, Dodo Payments processes the refund immediately back to your original payment method (credit/debit card, UPI, or wallet). Depending on your bank or card issuer, funds typically appear on your statement within 5 to 10 business days.

07. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

If you see an unfamiliar or unexpected charge from Dodo Payments / Kinich, please contact kinich.app@kinich.app first before filing a credit card chargeback with your bank. We resolve billing issues and eligible refunds rapidly (usually within 24–48 hours), whereas bank chargebacks take weeks and automatically trigger a temporary security freeze on the associated Kinich account while the dispute is investigated.

08. Free Tier & Guest Mode

The Kinich Free Tier and Guest Mode are completely free ($0) and never require a credit card or payment method. You will never be charged unless you explicitly select a paid plan and complete checkout through Dodo Payments.

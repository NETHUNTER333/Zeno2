01. What Kinich Does

Kinich (https://kinich.app) is an AI-powered research and study platform offering real-time AI search with cited web sources, autonomous Deep Research reports, a YouTube Study workspace (timestamped transcripts, structured summaries, interactive quizzes, flashcards, lesson plans, and audio summaries), persistent chat history, PDF and document study uploads, guest mode, user accounts via Email and Google sign-in, daily study streaks and achievement badges, a referral program with usage rewards, and optional paid subscription plans.

AI responses and study syntheses are generated using third-party large language model providers (including Google Gemini, DeepSeek, and OpenRouter/TokenHarbor-routed models). Subscription payments and taxes are processed by Dodo Payments acting as Merchant of Record.

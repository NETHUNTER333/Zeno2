01. How Kinich Uses Cookies & Browser Storage

Modern web applications use browser cookies and HTML5 localStorage to maintain session state across page reloads. At Kinich, we minimize browser storage to what is genuinely needed to authenticate your account, remember your interface preferences, cache your study workspace locally, and honor referral links.

We do NOT use third-party advertising networks, behavioral retargeting cookies, or cross-site tracking pixels.

03. How to Manage, Reject, or Clear Storage

You have full control over browser storage on Kinich:

  • In-App Cookie & Storage Banner: Use the "Essential Only" or "Manage" controls in our storage banner (or reopen it from the footer) to restrict non-essential functional/attribution storage.
  • Clear Guest Data Button: If you are using Kinich in Guest Mode, go to Settings → Profile & Account and click "Clear Guest Data" to immediately wipe all local chat threads, bookmarks, and preferences from your browser.
  • Browser Settings: You can block or delete cookies and site data at any time through your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, or Brave). Note that blocking Strictly Necessary storage will prevent Firebase Authentication from keeping you signed in.
